03 / Vulnerabilities

Critical vulnerabilities

Sourced from NVD (CVSS 7+), GitHub Security Advisories, and CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog — merged and deduped by CVE ID. EPSS (FIRST.org) scores each CVE's probability of exploitation in the next 30 days, independent of CVSS severity — use it alongside CVSS to triage "severe and likely" over "severe but unlikely." 🛡 marks CVE classes (SQL injection, XSS, RCE, SSRF, path traversal, deserialization, and similar request-shape bugs) a WAF or virtual-patch rule can typically mitigate at the edge — verify against the specific advisory before relying on it as your only control.
